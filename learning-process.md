# Learning Process

## Question 1: What is the Feynman Technique? Explain in 1 line.

The Feynman Technique is a learning method where we understand a concept deeply by explaining it in simple words, as if teaching it to someone else by exposing any gaps in your.

## Question 2: In the TED Talk by Barbara Oakley, what was the most interesting story or idea for you?

The most interesting idea for me was that taking breaks and doing different activities can help the brain understand difficult concepts better. Learning is not just about continuously studying; giving the brain time to process information is also important.

## Question 3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

- **Active (Focused) Mode:** Concentrating deeply on a particular topic or problem to understand and solve it.
- **Diffused Mode:** A relaxed state of thinking where the brain makes connections and processes information in the background, often during breaks, walking, or sleeping.

## Question 4: According to the video, what are the steps to take when approaching a new topic?

1. Deconstruct the skill.
2. Focus on one skill at a time.
3. Learn enough to self-correct.
4. Remove distraction to practice.
5. Practice at least 20 hours.

## Question 5: What are some of the actions you can take going forward to improve your learning process?

1. **Use the Feynman Technique:** To explain complex programming concepts simply in your own words.
2. **Manage Focus and Rest:** Balance focused work and diffuse thinking using the Pomodoro technique (25 minutes of deep focus).
3. **Minimize Distractions:** Eliminate digital distractions by keeping phones silent and blocking social media during work hours.
4. **Follow the Software Learning Framework:** By identifying where concepts apply and implementing them in code.
5. **Embrace Active Practice:** Prioritize active recall, repetition, and coding over passive reading or highlighting.

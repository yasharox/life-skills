# Prevention of Sexual Harassment (PoSH)

## Question 1: What kinds of behaviour cause sexual harassment?

Sexual harassment includes any unwanted behaviour of a sexual nature that makes another person feel uncomfortable, threatened, humiliated, or unsafe.

Some examples include:

1. **Unwanted Physical Contact:** Touching, hugging, or making physical advances without consent.

3. **Unwanted Messages:** Sending inappropriate texts, emails, pictures, or videos with sexual content.
4. **Unwelcome Advances:** Repeatedly asking someone for dates or sexual favours even after they have refused.
5. **Showing Explicit Content:** Displaying or sharing pornography or other sexually inappropriate material.
6. **Sexual Threats or Pressure:** Offering job benefits, promotions, or opportunities in exchange for sexual favours, or threatening someone for refusing.
7. **Inappropriate Gestures:** Making offensive gestures, staring, or behaving in a sexually suggestive manner.
8. **Creating a Hostile Environment:** Repeatedly engaging in unwanted behaviour that makes the workplace uncomfortable, intimidating, or unsafe.
9. **Inappropriate Comments:** Making sexual jokes, comments about someone's body, or using sexually offensive language.


Such behaviour is unacceptable, whether it happens in person, online, during office meetings, or at work-related events.

## Question 2: What would you do in case you face or witness any incident or repeated incidents of such behaviour?

If I face or witness any incident of sexual harassment, I would take the following steps:

1. **Speak Up:** If it is safe to do so, clearly communicate that the behaviour is inappropriate and must stop.
2. **Ensure Safety:** Move away from the situation and seek help if I feel unsafe.
3. **Maintain Confidentiality:** Avoid discussing the incident with unrelated colleagues or spreading rumours.
4. **Preserve Evidence:** Keep relevant emails, messages, screenshots, or other supporting information.
5. **Report the Incident:** Inform the HR team, Internal Complaints Committee (ICC), or designated PoSH representative through the official reporting process.
6. **Support the Affected Person:** If I witness an incident, I would support the affected person, listen without judging, and encourage them to report it.
7. **Document the Incident:** Record the date, time, location, details of the incident, and the names of any witnesses.
8. **Cooperate with the Investigation:** Provide honest information and cooperate with the official investigation if required.
9. **Avoid Retaliation:** Treat everyone respectfully and avoid any form of threats, pressure, or retaliation against the person who reports the incident.

I believe everyone deserves a safe, respectful, and inclusive workplace. I will never ignore inappropriate behaviour and will take responsible action if I face or witness such incidents.
